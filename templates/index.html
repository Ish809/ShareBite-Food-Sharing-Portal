OUR MISSION

Food Should Feed People, Not Landfills

Every day, good food is wasted while many people struggle to get enough to eat. ShareBite creates a simple connection between those who have surplus food and those who can distribute it to people in need.

01

Reduce Food Waste

Help restaurants and food donors prevent perfectly good surplus food from being wasted.

02

Support Communities

Make surplus food available to NGOs and beneficiaries who can distribute it where it is needed.

03

Build Connections

Bring donors, restaurants, NGOs and communities together through one simple platform.

SIMPLE PROCESS

How It Works

1

Post Surplus Food

Restaurants or donors provide details about the food they have available.

2

NGOs Find Food

NGOs and beneficiaries browse available food donations in their community.

3

Collect & Share

A request is made, the food is collected, and it reaches people who need it.
